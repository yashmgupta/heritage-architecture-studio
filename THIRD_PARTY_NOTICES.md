## Google Fonts

The HTML optionally imports **DM Sans** and **Manrope** through Google Fonts. These fonts are **not redistributed in this repository** and browser fallback fonts are available when offline. Review the upstream font licensing if proposing font bundling.

## CADCraft inspiration

Custom object picking, snapping, and move editing concepts were inspired by [CADCraft](https://github.com/storytold/cadcraft), an independent open-source CAD project. **CADCraft software or Rust/WebAssembly binaries are not bundled or copied** into this app.
