# Community Code of Conduct

We welcome learners, historians, designers, developers, and everyone curious about architecture. Be respectful, constructive, and considerate of different cultures and experiences.

- Be welcoming and discuss ideas rather than attacking people.
- Use specific evidence when correcting architectural or historical descriptions.
- Do not engage in harassment, insults, discriminatory remarks, threats, or deliberate disruption.
- Respect others' work, privacy, licenses, and cultural heritage.
- Give clear, actionable feedback; accept corrections gracefully.

The project maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation when these guidelines are violated. For ordinary moderation concerns, contact maintainers through GitHub Discussions or a public issue if appropriate. **Do not post sensitive personal details publicly.** For confidential concerns, use a private contact method provided by the project owner when one is configured.
